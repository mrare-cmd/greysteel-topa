How to update each week
  1. Open Weekly Reports. The newest DHCD report is at the top; if it says Not imported, click Import.
  2. Check the preview. New filings are ticked; anything already in the tracker is greyed out. Click Add.
  3. For new 5+ offers of sale, fill in owner, owner contact, owner address, listing broker and phone from CoStar - click the row in Filings and type into the side panel. Everyone sees edits within seconds.
  4. When a FOIA comes back, enter the buyer and seller on the row. The original value stays on record under the field.
  5. Import CoStar sale comps under Sales Comps; matching offers of sale are marked closed.
Address style (from the master list): street suffixes in full (Street, Avenue, Terrace), directions abbreviated (NW, NE, SE, SW). Imported addresses follow this automatically.
FOIA: DC FOIA Center. Request language: "Please provide the offer of sale notice recorded in the weekly TOPA report dated xx/xx/xxxx for [address]."
